About me

01

The image has to feel like a memory.

Stelios Dagkas is an Athens-based director and cinematographer working across narrative film, documentary, music, and commercial projects.

He is drawn to images that feel discovered rather than manufactured, light that behaves unpredictably, faces that reveal more than they intend to, and landscapes that become part of the story. His work balances careful composition with an instinct for the spontaneous, searching for beauty in the real, the intimate, the imperfect, and sometimes the uncomfortable.
